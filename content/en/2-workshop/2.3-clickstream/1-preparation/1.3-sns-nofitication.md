A complete automated system must have the ability to self-report results (Self-reporting). We will set up **Amazon SNS (Simple Notification Service)** to send an Email immediately when the data pipeline finishes running.

#### Setup steps
1. Access the **Amazon SNS** service, select **Topics** on the left menu and click **Create topic**.
2. Select the Topic type as **Standard**.
3. **Name:** Enter the name `Pipeline_Completion_Alert`. Scroll down to the bottom and click Create.
4. The Topic details screen appears, scroll down to the **Subscriptions** section and click the **Create subscription** button.
5. **Protocol:** Click the dropdown arrow and select `Email`.
6. **Endpoint:** Enter your personal Email address. Then click Create.
7. Open your Email inbox, find the email from AWS Notification and click on the **Confirm subscription** link to activate the channel to receive messages.
