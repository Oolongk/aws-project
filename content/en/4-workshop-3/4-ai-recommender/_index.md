Hello, welcome to **Chapter 4: Artificial Intelligence (AI) Layer**. After the Clickstream data flow has been cleaned, type-casted, and time-standardized to UNIX Epoch Time format through the Data Pipeline in the previous chapters, we have an Enterprise-standard "ingredient" ready for Machine Learning.

In this chapter, we will load this dataset into the specialized artificial intelligence service **Amazon Personalize**. The system will automatically learn interaction patterns and reader behavior matrices to build a real-time article recommender system. At the same time, we will apply a strict FinOps cost management discipline to optimize the operational budget.
